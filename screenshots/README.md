# Application Screenshots & Visual Tour

Screenshots of **Research Paper Trend Tracker** in action:

1. **Dashboard Overview**: Comprehensive KPI metrics, 14-day publication velocity line chart, category distribution bar chart, trending topics velocity table, prominent keyword cloud, and highest trend-score preprints.
2. **Paper Explorer**: Multi-criteria search and filtering by discipline, contribution type, prerequisite difficulty, date window, and trend score.
3. **Paper Detail Modal**: Full academic metadata, arXiv identifiers, original abstract vs. Gemini 3.8 Flash synthesis, and "Why is this paper showing up?" rationale.
4. **Trend Explorer**: Dynamic time-window velocity metrics (1d, 7d, 30d) and side-by-side topic comparison engine.
5. **Saved Papers & Personal Library**: Bookmark management with reading statuses (`unread`, `reading`, `read`) and editable personal notes.
6. **Executive Daily Research Brief**: Grounded statistical synthesis generated for researchers.
